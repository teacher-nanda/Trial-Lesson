3 · Practice

Same idea. Better English.

Choose the answer a fluent speaker would say.

1. You really enjoyed a movie. Which sentence sounds the most like a fluent speaker?

✓ “Absolutely worth watching” is a natural, stronger way to say it.

2. You didn't hear what your friend said. What do you say?

✓ “Could you say that again?” is polite and natural.

3. You don't know a word in English. What do you say?

✓ We use “How do you say… in English?”

4. A friend invites you out, but you're tired. Which reply is polite?

✓ Saying thanks + a reason + another plan sounds friendly.

Bonus

Four sounds, and you sound clear

These four make the biggest difference. Repeat after me.

1
The TH sound
Click to see it
think · this · three · brother
Tongue between your teeth.
2
Finish the word
Click to see it
book · work · night · big
End on the consonant. No extra vowel.
3
The H is real
Click to see it
hotel · house · happy · hello
Push air out.
4
The -ED ending
Click to see it
watched /t/ · played /d/ · wanted /id/
Three sounds, one spelling.
