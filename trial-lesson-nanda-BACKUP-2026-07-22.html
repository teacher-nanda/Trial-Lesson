Pronunciation

Four sounds, and you sound clear.

These four make the biggest difference. Click, then repeat after me.
1  The TH sound
think · this · three · brother
Tongue between your teeth. Not "tink", not "dis".
click to practice
2  Stop the extra vowel
book · work · night · big
End on the consonant. Not "booki", not "nighti".
click to practice
3  The H is real
hotel · house · happy · hello
Push air out. In English the H is never silent.
click to practice
4  The -ED ending
watched = /t/ · played = /d/ · wanted = /id/
Three sounds, one spelling. Most students never learn this.

Pronunciation

Four sounds, and you sound clear.

These four make the biggest difference. Click, then repeat after me.
1  Finish the word
desk · six · cold · milk
English words end on consonants. Don't drop them, don't add a vowel.
click to practice
2  The TH sound
think · this · three · brother
Tongue between your teeth. Not "sink", not "dis".
click to practice
3  V is not W
very · video · visit · TV
Top teeth touch your bottom lip. "Very", never "wery".
click to practice
4  The endings carry meaning
two books · he walked · she wanted
Drop the ending and you change the meaning. Most students never practice this.

Sound confident

It's not just the words —
it's how you say them.

Four small habits that make you sound sure of yourself. Click, then try it.
1  Slow down
Pause between your ideas. Silence is fine — it sounds calm, not nervous.
click
2  Stress the key word
"I'm really interested in this position." The stressed word carries your meaning.
click
3  End going down
Let your voice fall at the end of an answer. A falling tone sounds finished and confident.
click
4  Smile — they hear it
Warmth comes through your voice on a call. It's the easiest way to sound friendly.
click
